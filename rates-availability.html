Seasons & house rules

What the desert does, month by month

Rates hold steady most of the year. The weather does not, so here is the honest version before you pick a weekend.

01

Spring, March to May

Wildflowers, 75 degrees, and the busiest stretch of our year. Book six to eight weeks ahead if you want a Saturday.

02

Summer, June to August

Triple digits by noon, glorious after dark. The cabin is fully air conditioned and the hot tub runs cool in July. Weeknights are wide open.

03

Fall, September to November

Our favorite. Warm days, cold clear nights, best stargazing of the year. Fire pit season starts in earnest.

04

Winter, December to February

Thirties overnight, occasional snow dusting on the Joshua trees. Wood stove, extra blankets, and the quietest weeks we get. Holidays hold a three-night minimum.

Is the two-night minimum firm?

On Fridays and Saturdays, yes. Midweek we will happily take a single night if the calendar allows it. Ask and we will tell you straight.

What about cancellations?

Cancel more than 14 days out and you get a full refund. Inside 14 days we refund whatever we can rebook. Weather closures on the park road, full refund, no argument.

Dogs?

One well-mannered dog is welcome for $40 per stay. The property is fenced on three sides, not four, so keep an eye out for coyotes at dusk.

How far to the park entrance?

About nine minutes to the West Entrance station and five to the village for coffee and groceries. The last quarter mile of our road is graded dirt, fine for any car.
