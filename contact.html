Arrival & house rules

The things
people ask
before they come

If your question isn't here, call or write. We would rather talk it through before you book than sort it out at 10pm on a dirt road.

See rates
How do we get in?
Keypad on the front door. We text the code the morning of arrival along with a photo of the last turn, since the mailbox is easy to miss after dark. Check-in from 4pm, check-out by 11am.
What's the road like?
Paved to within half a mile, then graded dirt. A sedan handles it fine at 15 mph in dry weather. After heavy rain there can be washboard and a soft patch or two, and we'll tell you if it's rough that week.
Is the hot tub always on?
Yes, year round, kept around 102°F. In summer we can drop it a few degrees if you ask. It's cleaned and rebalanced between every stay.
Can we have a fire?
In the steel ring on the gravel pad only, and only when San Bernardino County isn't under a burn restriction. We leave a starter bundle of wood. Douse it before bed, no exceptions out here.
Quiet hours and neighbors?
Quiet from 10pm to 8am. Sound carries a long way in the high desert. No parties, no events, no amplified music outside. Four guests is a hard cap, including day visitors.
Dogs?
Two well-behaved dogs are welcome with a small pet fee. The yard isn't fenced and there are coyotes, so keep them leashed after sundown.
Cell service and wifi?
Starlink wifi is solid enough for video calls. Cell coverage is spotty depending on carrier, so download your maps before you leave the highway.
Minimum stay and changes?
Two nights on weekends and holidays, one night midweek when the calendar allows. Plans shift; write to us early and we'll do what we can with the dates.
