The place

A homesteader shack that refused to fall down

The original 1954 jackrabbit homestead sat empty for years with a sagging roof and a wall of packrat nests. We spent two winters putting it right: new beams, salvaged pine paneling, a wood stove that draws properly, and a deck built to face east so the sunrise wakes you up before your alarm does.

Nothing here is styled for a photo shoot. The mugs are mismatched, the record player skips on one album, and the good chairs are the ones by the fire pit. Guests keep telling us they slept nine hours straight. We believe them.

"Turned the phone off Friday night and forgot where I put it until Sunday." Dana R., stayed in March

The facts

Exactly what you get

No surprises on arrival. If something matters to your trip and it isn't listed here, ask us and we'll tell you straight.

Sleeps
4 guests, two bedrooms. We don't allow extra guests or day visitors.
Beds
One king in the front bedroom, two twins in the back bedroom. Wool blankets, cotton sheets, blackout curtains in both rooms.
Bath
One full bathroom with a tiled walk-in shower and good water pressure from our own well.
Hot tub
Four-person tub on the back deck, heated year round, cleaned between every stay. Towels are in the deck box.
Fire pit
Steel ring with six chairs around it. First bundle of wood is on us, more in the shed. Fires allowed unless the county says otherwise.
Stargazing deck
Raised platform east of the cabin with two loungers and a red-light lantern. All exterior lights are on switches so you can kill them completely.
Kitchen
Full kitchen: gas range, full fridge, coffee grinder and French press, cast iron, and a grill on the porch.
Internet and work
Satellite internet that holds a video call, plus a desk by the west window. Cell service is spotty for most carriers.
Heat and cooling
Central AC for summer, a wood stove and mini-split heat for winter nights, which get cold out here.
Dogs
One well-behaved dog welcome with advance notice. The land is unfenced, so leashes outside.
Parking and access
Gravel drive with room for two cars. Paved road to within a quarter mile, no 4WD needed.
Rate and stay length
Around $180 a night. Two-night minimum on weekends. Check-in 4pm, check-out 11am, self check-in with a keypad code.

Occasions

For the weekends that have a reason

01

The Proposal Night

Fire lit before you turn onto the drive, a bottle chilling, and the deck cleared down to two chairs and a blanket. Lights off at the switch so the sky does its part. You handle the question.

Anniversary, Quietly

Extra pillows and a body pillow on the king, decaf and herbal tea stocked, groceries in before you arrive so you never have to drive into town. We'll skip the hot tub talk and point you to the shady hikes.
